WELCOME TO THE FARM MODULE

Grow potatoes on Mars.

A tracked machine swaps powered heads to prepare, plant and harvest potatoes in a Martian habitat.

HOW TO PLAYSCROLL TO READ ↓
D-PAD / LEFT STICK: SELECT · SPACE ACTIVATE · RIGHT STICK / WII + & −: SCROLL

Move with WASD or the touch joystick. Orbit with QE and zoom with +−. Hold SPACE or Work to follow blue markers and work plots; release after each objective. Change tools with 1234.

  1. 1. Till · Tool 1

    Loosen the soil to give the potatoes room to grow.

  2. 2. Furrow · Tool 2

    Use the plow to open the planting rows.

  3. 3. Drill · Tool 3

    Use the auger to make holes for the seed potatoes.

  4. 4. Plant by hand

    Work each plot to take a seed potato from the machine's bin and plant it.

  5. 5. Water

    Park the machine with F or Park tool. Walk to the white cabinet and press SPACE or Work at its switch.

  6. 6. Hill · Tool 2 again

    Pick up the machine and select the plow again. Mound soil over the shoots to protect the potatoes from light.

  7. 7. Water again

    Park the machine and use the cabinet switch when the growing tubers are ready.

  8. 8. Harvest · Tool 4

    Pick up the machine and use the lifter to harvest. Select the tiller to start another crop; your total is kept.

At night, approach the habitat door and press SPACE or Work to rest and speed up growth.

This concept demonstration uses accelerated time and growth; its environment and farming systems are imagined.

This concept imagines a pressurized, climate-controlled greenhouse connected to a habitat. The growing medium is assumed to have already been treated for perchlorates and amended with nutrients; untreated Martian regolith is not ready for planting.

Recycled water, root-zone irrigation, supplemental lighting, power, and life support are essential dependencies. These are conceptual assumptions from the supplied research, not a validated Mars farming plan.

Grow lights illuminate the rows inside the dome. The accelerated sky approximates an equinox on Mars: Phobos rises in the west and Deimos in the east. Moon positions are illustrative, not predictions for a real date; rest remains available from 19:00 to 06:00.

Twenty plots and accelerated work keep this a short experience. The powered heads till, furrow and hill, bore planting holes, and lift the harvest; the sealed caddy protects their couplers from dust. Seed potatoes are stored beneath the caddy and planted by hand.

Park the machine and use the switch on the white cabinet to run the sprinklers for five seconds after planting and again during tuber growth. Automatic watering between these two checkpoints represents recurring irrigation, not just two waterings for the season. Growth takes 30 seconds per plot, paused at checkpoints until the work is done; night rest advances it by up to 15 seconds.

Progress saves automatically in this browser on this device. It does not sync between devices and may be removed if site data is cleared. The mission panel warns you if saving is unavailable.

Optional haptic feedback responds to movement and fieldwork on supported phones and controllers. Availability depends on your browser and device; this setting lasts for this session.

On Earth, potatoes need consistent moisture, especially during tuber development. The University of Minnesota Extension recommends roughly an inch of water per week and watering once or twice weekly, more often in sandy soil. A Mars habitat would need moisture sensing and site-specific irrigation; this demo does not model water quantities or prescribe an operating schedule.

The astronaut's walking animation is illustrative, not a physical simulation of movement in Mars gravity. The low-profile emergency suit is a visual concept, not a validated pressure suit. The rover, soil and crops are likewise simplified.

Controller icons: Input Prompts by Kenney (CC0).

Hold SPACE or Work to approach and service eligible plots; release and press again after an objective completes. Use F or Park Tool to leave the machine; return to its handle to pick it up. At the white cabinet switch, press SPACE or Work to start irrigation. At night, use the same control at the habitat door to rest. Change attachments with 1234, orbit with QE, and zoom with +−. On touch devices, drag the joystick to move, swipe the view to orbit, pinch to zoom, tap a plot to target it and hold Work. Open Controls for alternative on-screen buttons. Keyboard users can also use arrows to pan, C to recenter, I and K to tilt, and the mouse wheel or trackpad to zoom. A gamepad D-pad also moves. Hover or focus an attachment for its purpose.
